Every listing is checked before you see it.

Wrong phone numbers waste the time you don't have. So nothing goes up on Beacon until two separate checks agree and a person signs off — and then we check again on a schedule.

  1. 01
    Found
    A lead comes in from a directory, a partner, or someone like you.
  2. 02
    Researched twice
    Two separate checks look up the phone, hours and address on their own.
  3. 03
    Evidence scored
    Official sources count most. If anything disagrees, it waits.
  4. 04
    A person approves
    Nothing is published until a human reviewer signs off.
  5. 05
    Checked again
    Crisis lines are re-checked monthly; everything else on a schedule.
